### a principle that governs sustainability in complex free market systems

The moment communication stops, the basis for free exchange stops in the free market.

If institutions respond with disengagement from communication following legitimate, but challenging analysis of public representations, if disclosure questions are routed into complaint procedures, if procedural compliance is substituted for substantive review: then each of these is not only a retreat from the fiduciary responsibility of transparency, but is also a withdrawal from the only non-adversarial path that produces accurate disclosure. 

The principles of Free Market Exchange depend on continuous, evidence-based dialogue regarding the correspondence of representations to reality. 

This dialogue itself, conducted with technical rigor and documented accuracy, is the mechanism through which systems become more transparent and consumers become more confident. 

This is the only durable path to sustainable growth in the Free Mercantile Exchange and Free Market Capitalism.
