## I found that in the America of today, there existed the possibility that this defining tradition of American commerce may have been downgraded:

![](https://github.com/Aarception/Mo-information-security-portfolio/blob/main/IMG_20261126_152950.png)

Since the day I found out, I committed to repair.

The experience was documented along the way.

What has that experience taught me?

The entire incentive is upside-down. Which means intention, ethics, principals are all irrelevant. The system just does not view the consumer, the citizen, the public as the object of satisfaction, e.g. "The Customer Is Always Right." It organizes with ambivalence toward them.  This is why optics, actions, signaling are all controlled for 'plausible-deniability.' It is not that it is 'false' r 'superficial' as they are also irrelevant, since intent is irrelevant. An output has to be plausibly-deniable, because the target is subject to ambivilence. Understand what this means. It means the system will perfectly reflect every compliance requirement, while doing everything against the spirit of that requirement that is not constrained. One of the most surreal discoveries through this experience is the degree of digital infrastructure used to respond to that ambivalence against the target. One sees nothing, no movement, no suspcion-triggering outcomes, nothing; except, having been documenting with contextual awareness, there is an entire operation in the negative space, in the source code, in the algorithim. The system cannot be fair, or just, or even decent, as those things are irrelevant, because the incentivization is ambivalence toward the target. It tells me there has to be a totally new understanding about how this impacts concerns of society in this age. Imagine everything I have talked about, everything underlying this engagement, everything that precipitated it, the goal is still how to 'overcome' me. And then you think how can that even be possible or rational, until you realize I am the target of ambivalence, so rationality is irrelevant, ambivalence toward me is the incentive.

Data is the issue. Consumer Data. 

There needs to be a new paradigm understanding about the role of Consumer Data in the Algorithmic economy.

In the old economy, one enters a store, transacts with a business, buys an item, receive a copy of a receipt and then departed.

Now there is data, cookies, telemetry, intrusive mechanism, controllership.

Now one doesn't just depart with only a purchase and a copy of a receipt leaving behind a few bucks.

This issue has been rising at every new development, but there seems to be an inflection point now with AI and training data.

There is even a sense of covert escalation in how the platform is preparing to assert that 'ambivalence' toward the target.

This is no way to do business in modern America.

This is a deviation of culture being exhibited and it needs to be reincentivized positively.
